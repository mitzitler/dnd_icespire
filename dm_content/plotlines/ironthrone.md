## The Ghost of the Iron Throne

### The Iron Throne
This was a chaotic / evil aligned mercenary / highway robber / merchant group based out of Eastern Faerun, near Cormyr. Their sigil was an abstract gray throne. At the start of the campaign, this organization is mostly on the decline. But their lingering effect does have a hold on the region, and may work as a red herring in certain plotlines.

Not only does the Iron Throne's presence predate Cryovain in the region, it also most likely predates Cryovain's birth.

### The Hint in the Dwarven Excavation
The player's will find a crude map in the Hall of Greed, which originally was an Iron Throne map of Icespire Hold. When the players learn more about the Iron Throne, they will learn that this map must have been for the previous inhabitants. 

The orcs they encounter will have a faded Iron Throne sigil on their capes. If any orc is asked what it means and who it belongs to, they will say that this sigil has always been theirs as long as they could remember - this is their father's sign, and their grandfather's sign. As far as they know, it doesn't mean anything, and they dont have any concrete words to describe it either.
