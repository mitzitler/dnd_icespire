## Dopio MonDieu

### At the start

At the start of the campaign, Dopio has button-eyes, his enchanted mask, and normal shoes. He needs to acquire dancing shoes he can atune, and he needs to gather information on the assassination (attempt?) of the Duke Y'tar in Neverwinter.

### Secret Goal
[He is the treasure hunter](../secret_goals/1treasure_hunter.md)
He needs to have opportunities to sabotage the crew in order to further his quest. He is aiming for a roll of 30+.
