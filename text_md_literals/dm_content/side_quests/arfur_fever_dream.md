### Fever Dream

Arfur opens his eyes to discover he is in the middle of a field at a crossroads. Even through there is grass, dirt, bird song and sky, he can see in the distance that he is enclosed by steel walls on all ends, as if he were in a room. 

Next to him, on the ground, asleep, is someone from his waking life, a friend or campanion - who is it?

Roll a d6. If arfur gets a 1, he can choose between Al or Dopio. If he gets a 2, he can choose between Eve or Gasshole. If he gets 3-6 he can choose any of them.

They can choose to go north, south, west or east. These are the rooms:

They start in 13. No matter what room they go to next, they are told by some statue or figure:

> What a powerful dream you are having... Why, you may learn something new. Or perhaps something old? Do not try to chart every corner of this maze, as you equally must not chart every corner of your waking life. If you should fall here, and be unable to get up, fear not, you will find yourself in a familiar inn, past the worst of your illness.

In each room, roll a d20. If they get 18 or higher, they will find a small healing potion in the room.

In each room, the blue door is the northern door, the green door is the western door, the red door is the eastern door and the yellow door is the southern door:

        BLUE
    GREEN   RED
        YELLOW


#### Top row

##### 1 - Castle Corner
(exit)

The terrain of the room you've just left fades easily into the relaxed stone of the room. It's as if everything was very loud for a long time, and you only just noticed because you arrived somewhere calm. This room feels like fire-warm sheets and smells like fresh bread. In the corner you came from, there is a red door and a yellow door. However, in the opposite corner, the stone is crumbling away to reveal a familiar place. Stonehill. Theres just enough room for you to crawl through, if you want. If you squint, its almost as if you can see yourself, tossing and turning, beyond the crack in the wall.

##### 2 - Cursed Items
(prophecy)

(if they've been to the dining hall room)
Somehow, it is as if you are in the dining hall room from before.

You are in a dining hall, set for a lavish feast. However, no one is feasting. At the table settings there are empty robes and slips, as if the people at the feast all vanished, leaving their garments behind. The food is rotted. It has sat out for a long time. In the middle of the table, instead of large bowls of stew, roast pigs, or bottles of wine are objects. Magical objects, brimming with excitement. There's a dagger, a ring, a helmet.. items placed nicely in a row

For a DC 12 insight check, you can feel a malevolant energy.
For a DC 12 perception/investigation check, you notice that the chairs are upholstered with a design that looks very similar - eeriely similar - to that of the cape on the leader orc you saw at the excavation. For a DC 12 History check... you notice that even though this is for sure the Neverwinter region, the place settings, the utencil design, the threading on the robes - all of it is reminiscent of Cormyr - all the way on the eastern side of the continent in the Eastern Heartlands. 

##### 3 - Sleeping Manticore
(combat)

You are on a hill underneath a broken windmill. You have been here before! This is where you saved the woman Adabra, where you had your first adventure with your new friends. You feel your heart skip a beat as you see the manticore, from the first time you were here, is still here. Alive. She is asleep. She is heavily injured. You know that the other doors are inside the windmill. You make may a steathy move to try to get past the manticore in order to move into new areas. 

(There is no blue door, there is green red and yellow doors, the two that you didnt come from are the ones inside the windmill)

##### 4 - Word Room
(puzzle)

This room is a dirt plot with no doors at all. It is perfectly square. Though there are walls, there are also patches of grass, small rock piles, and a stamped out campfire.

Where there may be a door (east, west and south walls) instead in the dirt is written something in common: "ONE WORD". At another area, its 7 metal letters that can be picked up, also saying "ONE WORD". Finally at the last area, its 7 toy blocks, spelling out "ONE WORD".

If any are rearranged correctly, a NEW DOOR appears. A hint can be given by small tremors, which may rearrange one of them into: NEW ODOR and a foul smell occurs.

##### 5 - Zhent Plotting
(distant past)

You walk into a room that only has one other door. You are in a dark, dank pub, but it is as if the rest of the room doesn't exist: only a small table behind a red curtain with a suspicious woman. She has dark hair and a gravely voice. She seems very familiar - perhaps someone you've seen in Phandalin - but you are sure you are not in Phandalin right now. Neverwinter? Maybe even Waterdeep? People of all sorts slink in behind the curtain, have small, tense conversations, and slink away. They do not notice you, which gives you some comfort as you realize the other door is behind her table. 

When you walk past, she is speaking to someone with a large hood. You realize it is a half-orc woman who gives the impresion of a mad acolyte. They exchange money and maps. The human woman emphasizes that she needs instability in the region in order to sow chaos and create opportunities. The half-orc woman responds that she worships chaos for her own reasons, she refuses to disclose more. She promises that, with these resources, she and her sisters will sow the "storm's true destruction". Additionally, she has significant sway over the Orcs along the Triboar trail, and will rile them up as well. They compare it to "shaking the bubbly flask".

Finally, the human woman says, "Thank you, Yargath". The door appears.


#### Second row

##### 6 - Duke Y'Tar
(distant past)

You walk into a lavish hall and know intuitively that you are in Neverwinter. In walks the old duke and his son Lionel - you don't know these people, but you do know them. Lionel is a man now - was a man. He's the duke who was recently assassinated in Neverwinter. But he's so young in the vignette! You watch the pair strutted quickly across the room, the father scolding the son about something. They clearly don't notice you, and you realize that here you are just an observer.

Then you catch something in the corner of your eye. A fluttering. And you hear a rumble, almost like the stone itself is rumbling. It feels as if it's a dream effect, but then the boy turns at the sound of the rumbling as well. Something old and cracked in spoken from the shadows to the boy. He looks concerned. Young Lionel Y'Tar goes pale and clammy - and you see the fluttering of large, infernal wings. He feints, and as he hits the ground everyone is gone - father, son and presence. In front of you are three doors - blue red and yellow - one of which you just came from.


##### 7 - Phandalin Town Square
(waypoint)

You emerge into the middle of Phandalin town square. It is totally empty. There are some houses you dont recognize, and you see a red, yellow, green and blue house with some very dreamy looking doors - clearly paths to the next rooms. Besides that, the town square looks nearly as usual. The one exception: the stone shrine, typically in shambles, is totally torn down. In its place is a wooden post with a note on it. 

> Lady luck has left us. We must find our own way. Do not despair!

##### 8 - Mushroom Cave
(prophecy)

This room is not a room, but a ginormous dank cave. It has magically illuminated glowing orbs and mysterious mushrooms - purple, green and red. You see a small gnome working on a crossbow. She will not answer the players, but she will talk to herself. 

> Cryovain.. cryovain. I can't believe it. It's been centuries since.. wow. They called us crazy. And now, he's really here. Gods help us. He's really here.

You walk alongside a small stream towards three cave entrances - each one a color. Blue for north, green for west, red for east and yellow for south. As you get closer to the gnome girl, you realize the crossbow is MUCH larger than you first thought. 

Just before you enter the cave entrance into the next room, she calls out to the companion you are with:

> (Al)! Don't worry. I will get it done. Please believe in me. I will figure out how to make it work.

##### 9 - Sahuagin Encounter
(combat)

You emerge from a hatch in the ground onto a small island. There are two grotesque fishmen in front of you, which become immediately hostile. Behind them are three more hatches. 

You can do a DC 10 insight check to know that even though you are on an island, you are on an island in a lake. If you squint, you can see trees on the shore in many directions. If you get above a DC 15, you can see on one shore a dock and a dark blue tent.

You can do a DC 10 nature (or other int?) check to know that this is a Sahuagin, which are almost always ocean-dwelling, because they live in the depths and raid shorelines. To find them on a seemingly uninhabited island, in a forest-surrounded lake, is very odd.

Combat.

##### 10 - Assassin room
(puzzle)

You enter into a room that is medium sized and has the appearance of a basement. There are pillars and crates and the doors are hard to find. Like the room below you, there is only three doors - blue, green and yellow. You notice all at once that the room is full of people in the shadows. Assassins. It's impossible that they wouldnt have noticed you. Theres no way you could win in a fight against all of them, but they havent attacked you. 

If you get too close to any of them, they will all say at once: 

> Dont approach.

If the players notice that the assassins are only in the shadows, they can use light sources to create a path that the assassins wont be able to reach them in.

Alternatively, they can ask, why? And talk to the assassins with persuation checks to let them pass.

#### Middle row

##### 11 - Lava room
(puzzle)

There is a grand bridge which connects the platfrom you enter from to the platform which has the other three doors. As you enter, the bridge crumbles away. The platform is about ten feet above a river of lava. There is rocky crevaces all over the platforms leading into the lava, if you choose. There are stalagmites and ropes hanging from the ceiling, spaced far apart.

There are two ways across: either you walk slowly across the lava directly, or you pass increasingly difficult athetics checks to scale the walls via hanging and jumping off ceiling objects. This could be made easier in a wildshape. If they take the time to scout out the path, then they will get a preview into what checks are being asked:
DC 7, DC 12, DC 17

You notice that anything that falls into the lava crumbles instantly - whether its the bridge, falling rocks, or anything you choose to throw. However, some birds seem to be flying over the lava extremely low - almost as if they were skimming it. With an insight check, they will see the birds ARE skimming it. If they climb down the rocks they can try rolling a rock over the lava and they will see its only the FALL that triggers the lava. If they walk over it without breaking contact, they will be fine.

The players can also leave.

If they cross, they will see that two doors are ready for them and one of the doors was just a mirage. Depending on what direction they came from - 
Blue - north; Red - east; Yellow - south (there is not green - west)

##### 12 - Dining Hall
(distant past)

You see a large feast, full of figures wearing hooded robes. They are all shuffling food around, but not eating as far as you can tell.

If you approach them, you will see they are all ghosts. They dont see violent, but they dont seem friendly. 

They can't talk, but they CAN use sign language. If the team figures this out, they will understand this takeaway:

> We failed, we had to retreat. And our organization has all but disolved. Beware the curses we have left, tempting though they may be.

and:

> Our iron will was no match for our crumbling throne.

There are four doors in the room, including the one you came from. Blue (north), green (west), yellow (south) and red (east).

##### 13 - Grassy Crossroads 
(waypoint)
This is where they start. It seems like it would take forever to cross any of the paths, but it is as if in the blink of an eye they traverse the whole field.

There are blue flowers on the northern path, green flowers on the western path, yellow flowers on the southern path and red flowers on the eastern path.

##### 14 - Storm Room
(prophecy)

The room is small - almost a hut. There is a large metal rod in the middle of the floor which shoots through the ceiling. The hut is arid and made of wood, the furniture is made of wood, and the books on the shelves are made of wood. 

When they walk closer to the rod they hear an impossibly loud clap of thunder, and take 1d6 lightning damage. This will happen a second time in the room, and that time they will hear a booming voice as well:

> Hear me, chaos! My storm has only just begun! The thunder clap of destruction, the thunderous applause!

When they are leaving through any door, they will spot out of the corner of their eye a tiny wooden figurine of a board. If they return to this room it won't be there.

There is a blue, yellow, green and red door in the room.

##### 15 - Orc Encounter
(combat)

This room appears to be the edge of a cliff - there is no exit to the east, but there appear to be two paths along the side of the cliff face - one with blue bricks and one with yellow bricks. There are three orcs milling about that the characters must fight. Just like at the excavation site, the leader is wearing a tattered gray cape.

#### Fourth row

##### 16 - Twig blights
(combat)

This area is a heavily wooded forest, lit by the setting sun. You see some paces ahead of you what looks like the doors you've been seeing: they are colored archways, red blue and yellow - one of them being the one you just stumbled out of. 

As you walk slightly further, stumbling, you hear a rustle. For a DC 12 perception, you notice the ambush. Else, you are surprised by it.

Its a swarm of 8 twig blights. Harmless for the most part, but ganging up on you, they can be quite scary!


##### 17 - Possessed Dwarf
(puzzle)

You are in a grand palace and there is a dwarf you do not recognize in a possessed state. He is wearing a bright purple and gray cape which has the appearance of being on fire. Before rushing you - he screams - 

> Our will may be iron but it is no match for a crumbling throne!

There is a blank stare in his eyes. He is not present. The cape seems to ripple, not from the dwarf's movement, but from its own volition. 

The players must identify that the cape is the problem. Until they do, every attack will miss - on both sides. The player must either grapple or do a sleight of hand - anything inventive will do and take the cape off. The dwarf faints. The cape continues to writhe. It either needs to be tied up, set on fire, or ripped in half.

There are four huge palace doors in the room, one on each wall. A blue door, a red door, a yellow door and a green door.

##### 18 - Starry Courtyard
(distant past)

You walk into a courtyard where there sits a teenage woman. Her eyes though betray an oldness. It is a starry night with a huge nearly full moon. In the distance you see a huge mountain. If you squint (insight check) you can see smoke coming from the mountain. The woman looks at it forlornly. (This woman is the young version of Sister Garael, but arfur wouldnt know that). If you approach her, she will talk with you.

> It's a volcano. Did you know that? Not a mountain. I pray to my lady every night but some things.. cannot be avoided.

For a DC 15 history check, you know that this is Mt Hotenow, which erupted about 50 years ago, changing the region forever. 

For a DC 10 religion check, you know that this woman is a cleric - or training as one at least, as you can press her about her deity further, finding out that she worships Tymora.

Though you dont know it, this is actually a vista from the Hunting Lodge

> I fear truly for what is to pass. This volcano is not a disease but a symptom. We tend to see catastrophe as the cause - but it is almost always a reaction. I pledge my life to the victims of these symptoms, but I am just a leaf in the face of the disease. What can be done, but to struggle against the forces of illness?

She has nothing else to say, when you turn around to look for doors, you see that now the courtyard is closed on all sides, and you have four doors around you - blue green red and yellow.


##### 19 - Icespire Path
(waypoint)

You enter onto a freezing mountainside. There is a path, but it is narrow and rocky, and you worry about tripping. You will have to do two easy athetics checks or take 1d4 bludgeoning and 1d4 cold damage. You see ahead to the right a massive structure - a castle or a hold or a fortress. Closer, on the left of the path, is a guardhouse. If you enter the guardhouse, you will see the three doors.

##### 20 - Injured Centaur
(prophecy)

You stumble out onto a field which seems so much like the original crossroads, except there is no path to the east, and there is a very injured centaur lying in the middle of the crossed paths. He is despondent. You can leave him there, or if you are able to spare a healing spell or a potion, he will wake up enough to tell you:

> Thank you so much. There is so much trouble afoot here. And by here - of course I mean - there. Where you are. Where we are. Where we *really* are. Just stay focused. When the dragon came to these hills, it displaced everyone. Who knows how far this shockwave has spread? Who knows what has awakened, and what forces are at work? I need to talk to you. In person. Please stay focused. Your enemies - they are not alone. Not a single one of them is alone.

The centaur relaxes and falls asleep, clearly in less pain. Flowers bloom down the path to point to the north, east and south.

#### Bottom row

##### 21 - Harbin's Home
(prophecy)

You are inside someones home. It is cluttered but homey. There are some alchemical tools, and some caligraphy tools. A letter writing station and a banker's ledger. You have the eerie sense that you recognize this home, but also you are very sure you have never been inside this place before. A human man greets you. His voice sounds familiar, but you can't place it. And you don't recognize him at all. He offers you a plate of egg pasta and meat sauce. 

> I can't wait to see you all. You can't wait to see me. Who am I?

You are incapable of answering. But he's right - you are excited to see him, whoever he is.

He gestures you towards the doors in the room: blue green and red. (one of them is the one you came in from)

##### 22 - Ooze
(combat)

You are in the altar room at the excavation site. You see the Ooze this time, on the ceiling. You and your companion can stealth past it with advantage, a DC 15 check, because you know its tactics.

##### 23 - Color Path
(puzzle)

(By now Arfur will have figured out the door colors hopefully, though they may assume that the colors lead to the exit, and are not just cardinal directions)

You enter into a medium sized hall with a checkboard pattern floor. As you walk in, you see a rock fall in front of you from the ceiling and land on a tile. it glows faintly blue for a second, and then makes a pleasant sound, like a well tuned gong. Another rock falls onto the tile a few paces away but instead of a glowing, it does a harsh flash of white light and makes a sound like a gong breaking. The rock appears to be zapped with lightning from the tile, however that could be possible. There is no way to the other side of the room except to cross the checkerboard. On the other side, you see two doors. Behind you in the door you just entered from (there is no yellow door)

The checkerboard is a 7x7 grid. In the middle of the side closest to you (D7) there is a familiar wear on the stone, which makes it seem like a spot that has been stood on by many before. You stand on that spot and look to the grid and directly ahead, and see the same wornness on the stone on the other side (D1). In your hand appears a sheet of paper:

(need to draw this)
B  B  R  Y  R 
B  B  B  B  G
G  G  Y  G  G

If you step on the wrong spot, you take either 1d6 lightning or 1d6 psychic damage.
