### Necklace Hunt

**Session 2**

**For**: [Al B](../players/al_b.md) and [Eve](../players/eve.md)

**Given by**: [Pickled Pete](../../npc_content/all_npcs.md#pickled-pete-homebrew)

**Objective**: Retrieve a locket from [Marla Gon's](../../npc_content/all_npcs.md#marla-gon-homebrew) backyard. This locket was hidden behind rudimentary locks and hurdles, and contained a painting of Pete's younger face, but otherwise was insignificant.

**Result**: Pickled Pete gave the players some gold and vital information - they were told that [Harbin Wester](../../npc_content/all_npcs.md#harbin-wester) would be willing to sell them [Elixers of Cold Resistance](../../npc_content/magic_items.md#elixir-of-cold-resistance) at cost, once he trusts them enough. This will be very important for the fight against the dragon!

### Shopping Trip

**Session 2**

**For**: [Arfer](../players/arfer.md), [Dopio](../players/dopio.md) and [Gasshole](../players/gasshole.md)

**Given by**: [Carla Gon](../../npc_content/all_npcs.md#carla-gon-homebrew) (Marla's younger sister)

**Objective**: A casual shopping day with Carla takes a turn when she realizes she lost a packet of letters in a small patch of forest near town. The players must search the forest for the missing letters.

**Result**: The players found the letters. They read one of them, and saw that it was some correspondence between [Sister Garaele](../../npc_content/all_npcs.md#sister-garaele) and [Marla Gon](../../npc_content/all_npcs.md#marla-gon-homebrew). This is because they are both members of the [Harper](../../dm_content/plotlines/harpers.md) community in Phandalin. The players got three "IOUs" from Carla, and on the back of each one was a hint towards their specific public goals. How the IOUs will be used, we don't know!
