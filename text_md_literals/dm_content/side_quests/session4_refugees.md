### Manticore Encounter

For: All

Given by: Conyberry Refugee Family

Session: 3

On their way out of town for either Gnomenguard or the Dwarven Excavation, they encounter an overturned carriage and a dead mule. There are signs of a second mule, but with blood tracks dragged in a direction. Upon some investigation, the group will find a small family cowering nearby. They try to silently signal to the group that coming up behind them is... the manticore!

This is the mate of the manticore that they already slayed. This one has full health.

What happened [in session four](../session_diary.md#manticore-refugee-encounter):

The refugees revealed that they were in contact with the Harpers. Half the group - Gasshole and Dopio, chose a more materialistic path, whereas Eve and Al chose a more selfless one. Overall, they fixed the wagon and helped the three to Phandalin, and Gasshole & Dopio took a precious, delicate vase as payment. They have yet to find an appropriate vendor.
