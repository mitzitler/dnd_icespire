# The Ghost of the Iron Throne

## The Iron Throne
This was a chaotic / evil aligned mercenary / highway robber / merchant group based out of Eastern Faerun, near Cormyr. Their sigil was an abstract gray throne. At the start of the campaign, this organization is mostly on the decline. But their lingering effect does have a hold on the region, and may work as a red herring in certain plotlines.

Not only does the Iron Throne's presence predate Cryovain in the region, it also most likely predates Cryovain's birth.

## "Who are they"

They are a defunct organization - they are no one. However...

### Roaming Orc Raiders 

The adventureres will encounter roaming orcs in several quests and sidequests in this campaign. For the most part, all of the orcs have been dispaced by Cryovain. Before he moved into Icespire, THEY were the primary inhabitants. And Icespire is littered with "Thronic Artifacts" (see [Harpers](./harpers.md)). Their generations-old contract with the Iron Throne left them with the skeleton of this organization's structure, and it also left them to inhabit the last fortress in the area which had the Throne's influence. The artifacts have been scattered over the region along with the scattering of the orcs.

### "Why" are they?

So why were they here? Why do their remains, remain? The original goal was to indoctrinate the abundant orc population in the area, so that they could eventually use them as free bodyguards, highwaymen and merchant route security - like Bene Gesserits in Dune style. Their goals were cut short pretty early into their plans though, as unrest in the East forced them to retreat back to their strongholds, and abandon these ambitious long-term strategic plays.

But they left behind cursed items, politcal dogma, and a variety of traps - clever and crude. The Harpers aim to contain the threats and vestiges, as they have all quite recently come to light.

## The Hint in the Dwarven Excavation
The player's will find a crude map in the Hall of Greed, which originally was an Iron Throne map of Icespire Hold. When the players learn more about the Iron Throne, they will learn that this map must have been for the previous inhabitants. 

The orcs they encounter will have a faded Iron Throne sigil on their capes. If any orc is asked what it means and who it belongs to, they will say that this sigil has always been theirs as long as they could remember - this is their father's sign, and their grandfather's sign. As far as they know, it doesn't mean anything, and they dont have any concrete words to describe it either.

## [Cursed Objects](../../npc_content/magic_items.md#cursed-objects)

The Iron Throne, in their infinite misery and foresight, have left cursed objects in the region.

After discovering the sigil of the Iron Throne, though they have not realized it yet, the players may now start to find cursed objects in the region. Every day, roll a d4 to see if they may find a cursed object. If it equals 1, then they may encounter an object that day.

[Sister Garaele](../../npc_content/all_npcs.md#sister-garaele) is a cleric who can remove the curse of the objects, allowing the user to unequip and discard them. She will take the objects off their hands, for research.
