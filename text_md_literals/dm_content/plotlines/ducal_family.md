# The Old Duke Ytar

## Who is he?

The Old Duke Ytar **Llewelyn Y'tar**, not to be confused with his son, the Young Duke Ytar, **Lionel Y'tar** is a duke of some territory who prefers to spend his time in the big city of Neverwinter. 

He was assassinated, and his son takes his place. Or was he?

*Or was it, they were both assassinated (or were they) in the same coup?*

One of our players, [Dopio](../players/dopio.md), is being framed for the assassination. Another player, [Al](../players/al_b.md), is a former member of the guard corps which most likely undertook the assassination itself. However, it has been implied so far that:

- The assassination was unsuccessful - as shown in a note written in Theives Cant, deciphered by [Eve](../players/eve.md)
- The assassination has ties to the [Zhentarim](./zhentarim.md) network in the region
- The young duke is a warlock, with a powerful patron, seen in [Arfer's](../players/arfer.md) fever dream.
- The assassination was supposed to involve Al directly - and perhaps they even think he was actively a part of it - but because of his illiteracy he was oblivious to the plans.

## Who is he NOT?

He is NOT associated with the city of YARTAR, where [Linene Graywind](../../npc_content/all_npcs.md#linene-graywind) and the other Lionshields come from.
