## Town Locations

The players wake up each day in Stonehill, provided that they are not actively adventuring

### Stonehill Inn

Has food, drink, and storage chests. Costs 1 gp per session to stay here.

### Phandalin Miner's Exchange

Miner's stopping point to help sell items and requisition equipment. Secretely run by the Zhentarim. All mining, excavating, panning and logging advents are run through the PME such that Halia takes cuts and charts the area. 

### Barthen's Exchange

General store. Two young men work there - Anders and Thistle.

### Shrine of Luck

Sometimes, Sister Garaele will be there tending to the shrine and gathering the secret notes that are left there. She uses the public placement of the shrine as a way to observe the area.
