### General set up

Each player will get an envelope with three index cards. The first one will have a written summary of their goal (this will also be emailed). The second one will have, as needed, items that need to be kept track of. The third can be a general space for notes. At the end of each session, they can put their notes (and any other random items) into the envelope and the DM will hold onto it for the next session.

### Treasure Hunter
#### Summary
You have a credible source that there is a mithril ring somewhere in this region and you want to search for it, as you have heard it has some kind of impressive magical ability. NO ONE can find out that you are searching. You will do everything in your power to keep a normal or slow pace sooooooooooooooooooooooooooo oooooooooooooooo

#### What you need to keep track of:
- Whether you are aiming for a passive check of 25 or greater (19.5% chance), 30 or greater (5.25% chance) or 35 or greater (1.5% chance). 
- Write this choice down immediately, and hand it back to the DM when prompted.

#### What you need to do:
- The DM will roll a check for you automatically every new day, as long as you have indicated which passive roll is your target. If you are worried the DM forgot to do a passive roll, use the pass phrase: "My character brushes their teeth" or "My character quickly cleans the mud off the bottom of their shoes" (these do not take actions when done outside of combat)
- Divert your team. If you can get your party lost into a new hex when traveling, provided you are not moving at a fast pace, the DM will do another passive roll.
- If you personally search every room of the dungeon you are in, the DM will roll when leaving. (Use a code word to remind them!)

#### Conditions to reveal:
- Voluntarily
- When you automatically succeed the perception check
- When you are accused of intentionally trying to slow down the adventuring by 2 or more party members

#### Reason for secret:
- Not wanting to share the prize
- Worry that speaking about the prize will jinx the whole thing

#### Outcomes of revealing:
- If you succeed, you find the ring:
	- For a target of 25+, you roll the d6 and get an uncommon ring.
	- For a target of 30+, you roll the d6 and get a rare ring.
	- For a target of 35+, you roll the d6 with advantage, and may choose either rare ring outcome.
- If you give up your search, you give up the quest entirely.
