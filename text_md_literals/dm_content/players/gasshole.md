# Gasshole Flaps

## At the start

At the start of the campaign, Gasshole Flaps likes the finer things in life: good food, good wine, a good sneak-around - and even a good *encounter* with a stranger.

## Public Goal

Gasshole is a foodie, and his favorite restaurateur is supposedly in the region. This person is very secretive, but it will be very easy to identify them by trying their siganture dish: **Underdark Truffle Risotto**. After completing the side quest, [shopping trip](../side_quests/shopping_trip.md), Gasshole gets a clue in the form of a receipt:

> Several lbs of Menzoberranzan truffle

If this doesn't immediately click for him, he will do a history check and learn that Menzoberranzan is major city in the underdark.

## Secret Goal
He is avenging his father, searching for [his father's killer](../secret_goals/2avenging_the_murder.md)
