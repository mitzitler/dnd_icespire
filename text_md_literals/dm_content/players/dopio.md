# Dopio MonDieu

## At the start

At the start of the campaign, Dopio has button-eyes, his enchanted mask, and normal shoes. He needs to acquire dancing shoes he can atune. These will be *Thistle's Used Slippers of Study*, which can be used as a bardic focus (requiring attunement) and give a +1 to History checks.

## Public Goal

He needs to gather information on the assassination (attempt?) of the Duke Lionel Y'tar in Neverwinter. After completing the side quest, [shopping trip](../side_quests/shopping_trip.md), Dopio gets a clue:

> L'heur, Moy - Lionel east tot survit - DANC 54/CE <br> <br>
{an X with a crude skull in the top triangle, <br> the letters 'NW' in the left triangle, <br> a crude drawing of a pouch in the bottom triangle, <br> and two hollow dots on the right triangle} <br> <br>
Halia's signature, a cross through sign, PAS ACCOMPLIT

This note is written with the markings of Thieves Cant, and [Eve](eve.md) can decipher it for him. It says several things:
- The assassination failed
- Someone in town (the signature says Halia, but they won't be able to connect that until they meet her) is connected to the attempt
- The attempt took place in Neverwinter
- Payment for the assassination **happened anyway**

And finally, it's important to see that the note actually does not lean one way or another if it wanted to assassination to happen.
